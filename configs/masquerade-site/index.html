No. 03 / Walking
September 6, 2026

The path beside the water

The best part of an early walk is the stretch before the town is fully awake. At the bend in the path, the trees lean over the water and the sound of traffic disappears for a minute.

I took the longer way home. There was nothing particular to find, just the pleasure of seeing a familiar place at a different hour.

No. 02 / Reading
August 24, 2026

A place for unfinished books

There is a small stack by the window: an old collection of essays, a book about gardens, and a novel with a postcard marking the middle. I like moving between them without a schedule.

Sometimes a few pages are enough. The rest of the afternoon can belong to whatever those pages brought to mind.

No. 01 / At home
August 10, 2026

Rain at the open window

A brief afternoon shower changed the light in the room. I moved the plants closer to the window, made a pot of tea, and left the list of things to do where it was.

By evening the pavement was dry again. The room still smelled faintly of rain.
